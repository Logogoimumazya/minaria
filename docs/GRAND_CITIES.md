# Grand cities: proposal (nothing installed)

The user asked for more cities of King's Landing's scale and detail, as a plan to present later. This is that plan.
Nothing here is downloaded or placed until the user picks.

## What is openly licensed at this level

Of the KL-class builds, only two WesterosCraft releases are published for reuse, both **Creative Commons 4.0**
on CurseForge by geeberry, both in WesterosBlocks (already ported to 1.20.1 for Minaria):

| build | size | licence | status here |
|---|---|---|---|
| [King's Landing 2017](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/worlds/kings-landing-2017-version) | ~2,400 × 1,800 | CC 4.0 | placed (Austrum) |
| [Dragonstone 2013](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/worlds/dragonstone-2013-version) | island fortress | CC 4.0 | **downloaded, not placed** (`The WesterosCraft Modpack/saves/Dragonstone2013`) |

The rest of WesterosCraft (Oldtown, Lannisport, Winterfell and 300+ builds) is not released: the team keeps the
35 GB world on their server only ([FAQ](https://westeroscraft.fandom.com/wiki/Frequently_Asked_Questions)).
Minecraft Middle-Earth (Minas Tirith, Hobbiton) and similar projects publish showcases, not reuse licences;
they would need the authors' permission first.

## Proposal

1. **Dragonstone → the old throne on the Wound's island (Austrum).** LORE_MAP §2 puts the old empire's seat on
   the island in the middle of the Wound lake. Dragonstone is an island fortress of dark stone with dragon
   carvings: the ruin-haunted seat of a fallen empire. Same pipeline as KL (`stamp.py` + `seam.py`), same block
   set. Work: a stamp entry, a landform so the island rises from the lake, the seam, DT trees.
2. **Our own KL-class capitals from the KL piece library.** Aileau, Sykir, Lutesk and Stapledon are built by our
   generator at "city" scale. Raising one to capital scale (radius 330: 5 gates, suburb belt, full country)
   with its state's stone (walls.CULTURES) gives a second grand city with its own identity, no licence question.
   Lutesk (Rzhia, the forge province, the biggest city on the map) is the natural first.
3. **Ask permission** for one more reference city if the user wants a third hand-built one: candidates to write
   to are Minecraft Middle-Earth (a Gondor city for the Church's holy city) and Stone_Golem's
   [Realistic Medieval City](https://www.9minecraft.net/realistic-medieval-city-map/) (a Valarian capital);
   both need a written yes before use.
